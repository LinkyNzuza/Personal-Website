Introduction

I am a Digital Arts in Engineering student at University of the Witwatersrand within the School of Electrical and Information Engineering, majoring in Game Design through the School of Arts. My work combines creative and technical disciplines through game design, creative coding, 3D modelling, and interactive systems. I enjoy developing immersive digital experiences that merge visual storytelling with programming, electronics, and design. With a strong interest in both technology and creativity, I am passionate about building interactive projects that explore the connection between art, gameplay, and innovation. This website showcases my projects, experiments, and ongoing work within digital media and interactive design.

My Journey

Throughout my academic journey, I have worked on a wide range of projects that combine creativity and technical problem-solving, from electronics and circuit design to game development, 3D modelling, and interactive digital experiences, including the development of this website. These projects have allowed me to strengthen both my artistic and engineering skills while exploring different areas of digital media and technology. Alongside my technical work, I have also taken on various leadership roles throughout my schooling career, developing strong communication, teamwork, and organisational skills through collaborative projects and leadership opportunities.

Future Aspirations

I hope to continue developing new skills while refining and mastering the ones I already have. I am passionate about innovation and discovery, and I am always open to growth, new experiences, and opportunities that challenge me both creatively and technically. I enjoy exploring new ideas, experimenting with different technologies, and continuously expanding my knowledge as a designer and developer.
